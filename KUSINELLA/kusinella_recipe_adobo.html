Kusinella's Adobong Pusit Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs. medium-sized squid (note 1)
  • 1 piece onion (note 2)
  • 2 pieces tomatoes (note 3)
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 5 cloves crushed garlic
  • teaspoon sugar
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil

Instructions:

  1. Heat a wok or cooking pot
  2. pour-in soy sauce, vinegar, and water then bring to a boil.
  3. Add the squid and wait for the liquid to re-boil. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Turn off the heat then separate the squid from the liquid. Set aside.
  5. Pour-in cooking oil on a separate wok of cooking pot then apply heat.
  6. When the oil is hot enough, sauté the garlic, onions, and tomatoes.
  7. Place in the squid then cook for a few seconds.
  8. Pour-in the soy sauce-vinegar-water mixture that was used to cook the squid a while back.
  9. Bring to a boil and simmer for 3 minutes.
  10. Add the ink, salt, ground black pepper, and sugar then stir. Simmer for 3 minutes.
  11. Transfer to a serving bowl then serve.
  12. Share and enjoy!
