Kusinella's Fishball, Squidball and Kikiam Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 1 pack of Fishballs
  • 1 pack of Squidballs
  • 1 pack of Kikiam
  • Cooking oil
  • 2 tablespoon flour
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoon brown sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ⅛ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 small red chili (chopped)

Instructions:

  1. Warm a frying pan and add cooking oil.
  2. Fry your fishballs, squidballs and kikiam.
  3. Set aside when fried.
  4. In a saucepan, mix the flour, water, soy sauce, sugar, salt, and pepper.
  5. Bring the sauce to a simmer over medium heat. Continously stir.
  6. Add the chopped red chili and stir until the sauce thickens.
  7. Transfer to a bowl or a jar and let the sauce cool down.
  8. Serve the fishballs, squidballs and kikiam with the sauce
  9. Enjoy!
