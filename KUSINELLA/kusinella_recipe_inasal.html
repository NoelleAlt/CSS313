Kusinella's Chicken Inasal Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs chicken cut into serving pieces
  • 2 tablespoons ginger (minced)
  • 2 tablespoons garlic (minced)
  • 3/4 cup lemongrass (chopped)
  • 1 cup coconut vinegar
  • 1/2 cup lemon or calamansi juice
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 cup lemon soda
  • 1/2 tablespoon ground black pepper
  • 3 tablespoons annatto oil
  • 1/2 cup margarine (softened)
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon lemon or calamansi juice

Instructions:

  1. Mix lemongrass, ginger, garlic, sugar, vinegar, soda, and juice.
  2. Add chicken to the mix.
  3. Stir or shake the mixture until every ingredient is well incorporated.
  4. Marinade for 1 to 3 hours.
  5. In a bowl, combine margarine, oil, salt, and lemon juice then stir.
  6. Grill the chicken while basting generous amount of the margarine.
  7. Transfer the grilled chicken to a serving plate.
  8. Serve with sinamak. Share and enjoy!
