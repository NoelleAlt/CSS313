Kusinella's Longsilog Recipe

Ingredients:

  • Cooking oil
  • 1 cup of water
  • 2-4 cups of cold fried rice
  • 1 small onion / ½ onion
  • 5-7 Garlic cloves
  • 2-3 eggs
  • 5-10 pieces of longanisa (Filipino Sausage)
  • (Optional) Tomato
  • (Optional) Salted Eggs

Instructions:

  1. Chop the onion into thin slices / rings and set aside after.
  2. Mince the Garlic cloves and set aside seperate from the onions.
  3. In a pan, add some oil and wait until the oil is warm.
  4. Place the garlic in the pan and fry until garlic is slightly brown.
  5. Add the onion and fry until onion is slightly brown.
  6. Add the cold rice and stir fry until mixed well. Set aside afterwards.
  7. Fry the eggs sunny side up and set aside afterwards.
  8. Heat a seperate pan over medium heat.
  9. add water and place the longanisa in the pan.
  10. Bring the water to a boil.
  11. Continue to boil until the water evaporates while rolling the longanisa occasionally.
  12. Let the longanisa begin to fry in it's own oil.
  13. Continue to fry the longanisa for about 5 minutes while rolling to cook evenly.
  14. Keep doing so until longanisa is slightly crisp on the outside.
  15. Place the eggs, longanisa and fried rice in a plate and enjoy!
  16. (Extra) If you want to, serve and eat with sliced tomato and salted egg.
