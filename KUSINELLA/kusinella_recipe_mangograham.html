Kusinella's Mango Graham Recipe

Ingredients:

  • heavy cream, chilled overnight
  • sweetened condensed milk, chilled overnight
  • salt
  • Ripe mangos
  • Graham Crackers

Instructions:

  1. Peel the skin and slice (or chop) the mangos.
  2. Whip heavy cream until soft peaks form.
  3. gradually add pinch of salt and condensed milk.
  4. Whip cream until stiff peaks form.
  5. Place the graham crackers in an 8x8 glass container.
  6. Place whipped cream on top of graham crackers.
  7. Place sliced mango on top of whipped cream
  8. Repeat step 5-7.
  9. Cover container and refrigerate overnight.
  10. Serve and Enjoy!
