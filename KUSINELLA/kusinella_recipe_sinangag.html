Kusinella's Corned Beef Sinangag Recipe

Ingredients:

  • cooking oil
  • 1 can of corned beef
  • 2-3 eggs
  • 4 cups of cold cooked rice
  • 1 pinch of salt
  • 1 pinch of pepper
  • 1 small onion / ½ onion
  • 5-7 Garlic cloves

Instructions:

  1. Fry the corned beef and set aside when crispy.
  2. In a small bowl, beat the eggs and mix with salt and pepper.
  3. Fry the egg mix and stir. Cut into small pieces.
  4. Chop the onion into thin slices / rings and set aside after.
  5. Mince the Garlic cloves and set aside seperate from the onions.
  6. In a pan, add some oil and wait until the oil is warm.
  7. Place the garlic in the pan and fry until garlic is slightly brown.
  8. Add the onion and fry until onion is slightly brown.
  9. Add the cold rice and stir fry until mixed well.
  10. Carefully mix the fried eggs and corned beef into the rice.
  11. Once they're well combined, place them in a bowl and enjoy!
