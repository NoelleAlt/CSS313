Kusinella's Tortang Talong Recipe

Ingredients:

  • eggplants
  • eggs (same amount as eggplant)
  • salt and pepper to taste.
  • oil

Instructions:

  1. With a knife or fork, prick eggplant.
  2. Arrange eggplants over high flame and roast, rotate eggplant occasionally.
  3. Wait until eggplant is slightly softened and skin is evenly charred.
  4. Remove from heat and allow it to cool to touch.
  5. Peel the skin and rinse under running water to rid of stray skin.
  6. Flatten the eggplants but leave the stems intact.
  7. In a wide pan over medium heat, heat oil.
  8. In a shallow bowl, mix the eggs with salt and pepper.
  9. Holding by the stem, dip the eggplant in the beaten eggs and quickly slide into the pan.
  10. Cook for about 2 to 3 minutes on each side or until golden.
  11. Serve with ketchup and enjoy!
